Getting started in the studio

  1. Open Studio and choose Try an example or start drawing. You do not need an account or microphone access.
  2. Choose Strokes, Rectangles or Particles, the three free materials. Changing the material or color preserves your earlier strokes.
  3. Add layers to separate elements, adjust their opacity and use Undo or Redo.
  4. Wait for Saved to appear. In Works, you can reopen, duplicate or delete your projects.
  5. Replay the creative process, use Exhibit and share an image from Export.

Sound, photos and camera

Optional sound input changes the energy, color or stroke width of your artwork. Audio is analyzed on your device; the app does not interpret spoken descriptions or save voice recordings. You can pause listening and continue drawing.

Extract colors from a selected photo or the camera. Photo contours create editable elements. Images are processed locally. Camera and microphone access are optional, and you can manage their permissions in your device settings.

Saved works and exports

Your artwork is saved on this device. Captures from the earlier Aurio gallery are imported as images; their original strokes cannot be reconstructed. Export PNG images or a silent six-second MP4 video of the creative process.

Before deleting the app or changing devices, export artwork you want to keep. An image or video export is not an editable project backup. Deleting a work does not delete copies you have already shared.

Aurio Pro and temporary access

Aurio Pro is a one-time purchase that includes ten additional materials, photo contours, video export and ad removal. The current price is shown in the app.

If you already purchased Aurio Pro, use Restore purchases with the same Apple account. If restoration fails, check your account and internet connection, then contact support without sending payment details.

You can also watch an optional rewarded ad to access one selected feature for 30 minutes. Each material, photo contours and video export has an independent expiry time. Temporary access does not activate Pro or unlock other features. Your saved artwork is preserved when access expires.

Privacy and contact

No account is required. Your projects, microphone audio and photos are processed locally. The free version uses Google AdMob, and Apple handles purchases. Read our Privacy Policy for details. When available, Privacy options in Settings lets you review advertising consent.
